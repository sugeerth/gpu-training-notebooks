What the KV cache actually costs

Weights are a fixed cost you pay once. The KV cache is a running cost you pay for every token of every conversation — and it is the reason long context is expensive. The attention architecture decides how expensive, and the difference between designs is three orders of magnitude.

Sliding-window layers never store more than their window, which is why Mistral's line goes flat. MLA stores one compressed latent vector per layer instead of a key and a value per head — that is the whole trick, and it is why DeepSeek-V3 has a smaller KV cache than an 8B model.
