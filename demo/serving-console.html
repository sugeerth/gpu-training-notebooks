What this is

A port of the planning model from The What-If Console, which consolidates the equations derived across the serving track: KV bytes per token, the roofline for decode, TP topology forced by VRAM, kernel-maturity penalties for int4, and the engine's fixed per-step cost. Identity checks in the notebook assert the model reproduces those derivations exactly.

What it is not

A benchmark. Efficiency constants (75% of peak bandwidth, 60% of peak FLOPs, 0.8 ms of engine overhead) are defaults, not measurements of your box. Use this to shortlist configurations, then measure the shortlist open-loop on the real hardware and fold your numbers back in. Treat any single figure here as a ballpark, never a promise.
