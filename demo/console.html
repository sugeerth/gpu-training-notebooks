Resource ledger

What the pulled levers are spending. Two levers drawing on one resource is the mechanical reason their gains will not multiply.

The ladder

Levers added one at a time, in the order a team would reach for them. The cost column includes the accelerators a lever rents, not just the tokens it moves.

Interactions

Expected gain against actual. The why column comes from the levers' own declarations, so the conflict is predicted rather than discovered.

Control loop

Give it an objective instead of a question. It proposes a change, predicts what the change will do, measures, and reverts when the measurement disagrees — so the trace below is the answer, not the last row of it. The acc column is the share of the predicted improvement that actually arrived. It may change the accelerator, but only among the four the step budget models — every other card in the catalog is evaluated as the nearest one it knows, so offering it would be offering a decision this model cannot actually make.

 

Events

Every phase of the loop emits a structured event. These are the same events the service's log hooks receive — in the browser they come from the loop directly, and with an API connected they are pulled from /v1/logs, which is the service's own ring buffer. One stream, two sources.
