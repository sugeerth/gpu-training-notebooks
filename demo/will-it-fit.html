Will it fit?

The first question anyone asks about serving a model, answered properly: not just whether the weights load, but how many people can talk to it at once.

Weights

Two numbers decide this: the weights, which are fixed, and the KV cache, which grows with every token of every conversation. The KV cache is what actually runs out.
